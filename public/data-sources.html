Data sources and coverage

RailGo contains 10,516 unique five-digit train numbers, 9,494 stations and 289,363 published route entries. This is a combined public catalogue. It is not a verified list of trains running today.

  • 8,490 records: Indian Trains Schedule & Routes by Rohan, published September 15, 2025. Publisher-declared MIT license. Includes express, passenger and superfast files. Publication date is not a timetable validity guarantee.
  • 2,026 additional records: DataMeet Indian Railways archive, snapshot August 8, 2016, CC0. These older records may be renamed, renumbered or discontinued. DataMeet coordinates also provide station map positions. Some route entries may be passing points, with halt status unknown.

Duplicate numbers use the newer dataset. 69 non-standard identifiers were excluded. Missing coordinates, running days, times and distances stay unknown. We do not fabricate ratings, fares, seat availability, platforms or train positions. 1,106 stations lack source coordinates. Map lines connect stations; they are not surveyed rail geometry.

Places and real photographs

Tourist locations come from Wikidata (CC0 structured data). Photographs come from Wikimedia Commons. Each card links its original image, author, license and place source. Images are displayed as resized or cropped thumbnails under their stated license.

Places are matched by straight-line distance to every station in the selected route. Choose a station and a 10–100 km radius. Only places with available coordinates and attributed photographs are included. This is not an exhaustive list of attractions in every town. Distance does not indicate safe visiting time during a train stop. Directions open Google Maps from the relevant station.

The homepage photograph shows Bangalore Palace: SMit224, original image, CC BY-SA 3.0; displayed with a crop and dark overlay. No generated photograph is used.

Current services

Confirm current operation, timetables and running status with NTES. Check availability, fares and book valid railway tickets on IRCTC. RailGo is not an authorized ticket issuer and has no live railway provider connected.

Weather is requested from Open-Meteo for the destination coordinates. An outage shows unavailable values. Email verification uses a hosted backend and a configured Resend sender; a static hosting preview cannot deliver verification codes.

Machine-readable records: train import manifest, source metadata, tourism query and retrieval date, places and individual photo credits.
